# Authored lake; Water plugin is visual only

The Lake is a real map at the current playable radius (4500), with a sculpted bank, one island, and dressed trees/rocks. Auto-build primitives are not the ship look.

Water + Landmass (and Niagara) provide the Water Body Lake. Charge-cast hits, Boat float height, bobber, and fish depth still use the Z-plane `WaterHeight` contract. Waves do not drive gameplay.

Coupling fishing to water collision or buoyancy was rejected: that would rewrite the sequence this look exists to judge.
