# Stylized PBR art direction

The game should look like a shipped title, not a prototype, without becoming a fishing sim. We picked Fortnite/Overwatch-family stylized PBR: real silhouettes, readable heroic proportions, painted-but-lit materials, saturated daylight.

Photoreal, arcade-chunky bass, painterly gloom, and cute-flat were rejected. Assets that fight this (MetaHuman, photogrammetry nature, low-poly cute) are out of style.

This is expensive to reverse once the Angler, Boat, fish, and Lake are bought or authored to match.
